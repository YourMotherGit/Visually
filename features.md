# Features

This document provides a comprehensive overview of Visually's features.

## The Create Page (Visually Flow)

The heart of Visually is the **Create Page**, which features the **Visually Flow** editor. This is a visual, node-based canvas where you build the structure of your presentation. Instead of linear slides, you create a flow of interconnected content blocks, allowing for dynamic and interactive storytelling.

**How it works:**

1.  **Drag and Drop:** Drag different content nodes from the sidebar onto the canvas.
2.  **Connect Nodes:** Draw connections between nodes to define the presentation flow.
3.  **Edit Content:** Click on a node to open its editor and add your content.

## Content Nodes & Editors

### Slide Editor

The **Slide Editor** is your classic presentation tool, reimagined for Visually.

-   **What it does:** Create rich text slides with formatting options, lists, and links.
-   **How it works:**
    1.  Drag a "Slide" node onto the canvas.
    2.  Click the node to open the editor.
    3.  Use the WYSIWYG editor to create your content.

### Chart Editor

Bring your data to life with the **Chart Editor**.

-   **What it does:** Create various types of charts (bar, line, pie, etc.) to visualize data.
-   **How it works:**
    1.  Add a "Chart" node to your flow.
    2.  Open the editor to input your data, customize labels, and choose a chart type.

### Code Project Editor

Showcase your code with syntax highlighting.

-   **What it does:** Display code snippets in various programming languages.
-   **How it works:**
    1.  Use a "Code" node.
    2.  Select the language and paste or write your code in the editor.

### QR Code Editor

Engage your audience with interactive links.

-   **What it does:** Generate QR codes that link to websites or other resources.
-   **How it works:**
    1.  Place a "QR Code" node.
    2.  Enter the URL you want the QR code to point to.

## AI-Powered Features

Visually integrates powerful AI tools to streamline your creation process.


### Chatbot

-   **What it does:** A conversational AI to help you with your presentation. You can ask for ideas, summaries, or help with structuring your content.
-   **How it works:** Access the chatbot from the sidebar. Type your questions or requests and get instant AI-powered assistance.

### Presentation Refiner

-   **What it does:** Analyzes your entire presentation and suggests improvements for flow, clarity, and engagement.
-   **How it works:** From the "Review" menu, select "AI Refiner," and the AI will provide a report with actionable suggestions.

### Websight

-   **What it does:** Gathers information from a given URL and incorporates it into your presentation.
-   **How it works:** Add a "Websight" node, enter a URL, and the AI will fetch the content, summarize it, and format it for a slide.

## Media and Uploads

### Media Upload Form

-   **What it does:** Upload your own images, videos, and audio files to use in your presentation.
--  **How it works:** Access the "Media" tab, and you can drag and drop your files or select them from your computer.

### Open from Device

-   **What it does:** Directly open and edit presentations stored on your local device.
-   **How it works:** From the main menu, choose "Open from Device" and select your Visually project file.

## Other Key Features

### Diagrams

-   **What it does:** Build custom diagrams and flowcharts.
-   **How it works:** Use the "Diagram" tool to create and customize complex diagrams with various shapes and connectors.

### Document Editor

-   **What it does:** A collaborative, rich-text document editor.
-   **How it works:** Create or open a document and edit it in real-time with your team.

### Mind Maps

-   **What it does:** Brainstorm and organize your ideas with interactive mind maps.
-   **How it works:** The "Mind Map" feature provides a canvas for creating and connecting ideas in a hierarchical structure.
